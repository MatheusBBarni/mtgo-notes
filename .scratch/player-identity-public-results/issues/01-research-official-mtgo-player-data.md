## Question

What verifiable player information can currently be discovered from an exact MTGO nickname through official Wizards or MTGO public surfaces, and what documented access, attribution, stable identity, rate-limit, terms, and failure constraints govern each surface?

## Answer

No official global MTGO player-profile API exists. The documented Daybreak Census MOCS leaderboard is the only gated machine-readable candidate; official MTGO decklists remain an explicit browser/manual source until a documented contract or written permission exists.
