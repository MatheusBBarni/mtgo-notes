## Question

Which reputable third-party public sources can currently return verifiable MTGO tournament results or published decklists for an exact nickname, and what coverage, provenance, stable identity, access method, rate-limit, terms, and reliability tradeoffs would a privacy-first desktop client inherit from each?

## Answer

No surveyed third-party source offers a documented, permissioned, reusable machine-access contract for V1. MTGTop8 is the preferred optional external search, with other reputable sources limited to user-initiated browser corroboration under their individual constraints.
